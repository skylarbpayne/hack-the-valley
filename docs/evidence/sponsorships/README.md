# Local sponsorship acceptance evidence

Captured September 24, 2026 against the isolated local D1/R2 prototype.
All identities, businesses, payments and references shown are fictional.
The walkthrough adds one business and an archived 2028 campaign to the initial
fixtures; reset/setup returns to the original seeded data.

- [Weekly review](weekly-review.png): seeded outreach plus the saved demo business.
- [Commitments](commitments.png): cash settlement, outstanding balances and in-kind fulfillment.
- [Commitment details](commitment-details.png): $500 received, invoice/check references and private uploaded logo.
- [Reminder inbox](reminder-inbox.png): distinct owner summaries and deduplicated daily snapshots; preview only.
- [Narrow layout](mobile-review.png): 390px viewport, table scrolling stays inside its container.
- [Member denied](member-denied.png): ordinary members cannot load the workspace data.

The final walkthrough also verified shared contact edits across campaigns,
activity logging, owner/status/overdue filters, follow-up completion and
rescheduling, campaign archival, both admin roles, account switching, and saved
data/media after stopping and restarting the local server. See the
[prototype guide](../../sponsorship-prototype.md) for exact commands and limits.
