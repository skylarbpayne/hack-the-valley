Project Spotlight: NewGen Inspection Scheduler

An inspection can take an hour. Scheduling it, notifying tenants, interpreting replies, rescheduling conflicts, and turning the final report into repair notices can eat the rest of the day.

Jordan Garcia built NewGen Inspection Scheduler around that less-visible problem. The idea came from work in his family’s property management business, where a single inspection workflow is often split across calendars, text messages, spreadsheets, and report files.

One workflow instead of six tabs

NewGen is designed for small property management companies and independent landlords—the people most likely to feel this administrative drag and least likely to have a custom operations team.

The application brings the inspection workflow together:

  • Manage properties, owners, and tenants.
  • Schedule inspections across multiple properties.
  • Prepare SMS inspection notices for tenants.
  • Classify replies such as confirmations and reschedule requests.
  • Review AI-generated scheduling recommendations before changing anything.
  • Upload inspection reports for deficiency extraction and repair-notice drafting.

AI proposes. The property manager decides.

The important product choice is not the use of AI. It is where the AI stops.

NewGen can classify a tenant’s reply and suggest a new inspection window, but the recommendation lands in an approval queue. A property manager can approve it, reject it, or edit the time before the schedule changes. That keeps the repetitive interpretation work automated without handing operational judgment to a model.

The report is not the end of the workflow

After an inspection, the administrative work starts again. NewGen accepts an inspection report, extracts likely deficiencies, and prepares a repair notice for review. The manager still sees the source summary and identified items before anything goes to a tenant.

What comes next

NewGen is currently a demonstration environment. Jordan’s next major milestone is integrating Twilio or a similar service for live SMS communication. That is the step that would move the project from a convincing workflow prototype into real property-management use.

The goal is not to replace property managers. It is to strip repetitive coordination out of their day while keeping people in control of tenant communication, scheduling changes, and repair decisions.

“Demo Hours was my first opportunity to publicly showcase it, and I couldn’t have asked for a better experience.” — Jordan Garcia

NewGen combines AI, workflow automation, and user-experience design around a problem Jordan knows firsthand. That is what Demo Hours is for: put the work in front of real people, explain the decisions behind it, and find the next step.
